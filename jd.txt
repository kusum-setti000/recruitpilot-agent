Job Title: Senior Generative AI Engineer
Location: Reston, VA (Hybrid, 3 days onsite) or Remote within the US
Employment Type: 12 month contract with possible extension

About the role
We are a mortgage and housing finance organization building LLM powered assistants for underwriting, servicing and compliance teams. You will design, build and run production grade Generative AI applications, with a focus on retrieval augmented generation over large regulated document sets and agentic workflows that automate multi step analyst tasks.

Responsibilities
Design and build RAG pipelines over policy, guideline and loan documents, including chunking, embeddings, hybrid search and reranking.
Build agentic workflows with LangGraph or similar orchestration frameworks, including tool calling and human in the loop review.
Expose AI capabilities through secure Python APIs (FastAPI) and deploy them on AWS.
Own LLM evaluation: golden datasets, automated regression checks, hallucination and groundedness metrics.
Work with risk and compliance partners to put guardrails, audit logging and PII handling in place.
Mentor engineers and help set best practices for prompt engineering and LLM ops.

Required qualifications
7+ years of experience in software engineering, machine learning or data science, with at least 2 years building LLM applications.
Strong Python.
Hands on experience with LLMs, RAG and prompt engineering.
LangChain and LangGraph.
Vector databases (pgvector, Pinecone, FAISS or similar).
FastAPI or equivalent REST API frameworks.
AWS (Bedrock, Lambda, ECS or SageMaker).

Preferred qualifications
LLM evaluation frameworks such as RAGAS or LangSmith.
Fine-tuning or LoRA experience.
Docker and Kubernetes.
Experience in financial services, mortgage or another regulated industry.
Azure OpenAI exposure.
